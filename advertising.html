Advertising & cookies

Last updated: 2026-08-11

This page explains how the public website at verko.lt uses cookies and displays third-party advertising. It covers this website only. Personal data you give us as a Verko user — your account, listings, messages and bookings — is governed by the Verko privacy policy, which is linked at the bottom of this page.

Advertising on this website

verko.lt displays advertising supplied by Google, through the Google AdSense programme. The adverts are selected and delivered by Google, not by us, and we do not control which specific advertisers appear.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve adverts based on your prior visits to this website or to other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve adverts to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • These cookies may involve your IP address, device and browser information, and general location, and are used for ad selection, frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention.

How to opt out

You are not obliged to accept personalised advertising. You can:

  • Turn off personalised advertising in your Google account at google.com/settings/ads.
  • Opt out of third-party vendor cookies used for advertising at aboutads.info/choices or youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking them does not remove the adverts; it means they are chosen without reference to your browsing history.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent prompt is shown before advertising cookies that require consent are set, and you can change or withdraw that choice at any time through the same prompt.

What this website stores itself

Apart from advertising, verko.lt keeps very little. It stores two preferences in your browser's local storage so the site looks the same on your next visit:

  • your chosen colour theme (light or dark);
  • your chosen interface language (Lithuanian or English).

Neither leaves your device, neither identifies you, and clearing your browser data removes both. The listings shown on the home page are fetched from the Verko service so that the page can display them; browsing this website does not require an account and does not create one.
