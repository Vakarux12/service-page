About

What Verko is, and why it exists.

Verko is a peer-to-peer marketplace for everyday services in Lithuania. If you need a flat cleaned, a tap fixed, a sofa carried up three flights of stairs or a lawn cut, Verko lets you find someone nearby who does that work — and talk to them directly.

The usual way of booking household help puts an agency in the middle. The agency takes the call, assigns whoever is free, adds its margin, and you never really know who is coming or how much of your money reaches them. Verko removes that layer. You see the actual person, their price, their past reviews and their coverage area before you commit to anything.

It works the same way in reverse. If you clean, repair, move, tutor or garden for a living, you publish your own listing, set your own rate and choose your own hours. The reputation you build stays attached to you rather than to an employer, and the customers who find you are people who searched for exactly what you offer.

Verko is built and run in Lithuania, and the marketplace covers Lithuanian towns and cities. The website you are reading is the public front door; browsing, messaging, booking and reviews all happen in the Verko app.

Cleaning

Regular flat and house cleaning, one-off deep cleans, cleaning after a tenancy ends, and post-renovation clean-ups where dust and paint residue need specialist attention.

Safety

Hiring a stranger, sensibly.

Before you book

1

Read the reviews, not just the score

A rating averages everything. The written reviews tell you whether someone turns up on time and how they handle problems.

2

Agree the scope in writing

Say what needs doing, how long you expect it to take and what the price covers — in the chat, before the work starts, so both of you can point back to it.

3

Keep the conversation on Verko

If a dispute comes up later, a conversation that happened in the app is something you can both refer to. Be wary of anyone who wants to move the deal elsewhere immediately.

Warning signs

!

Pressure to pay everything upfront

A deposit for materials is normal. Demanding the full amount before any work has started, especially by instant transfer, is not.

!

A price that makes no sense

An offer far below every comparable listing usually means the scope is narrower than you think, or that costs will appear once the job is underway.

!

Reluctance to be identified

Verko accounts are phone-verified and each profile carries a legitimacy score. Someone avoiding basic questions about who they are is telling you something.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Does it cost anything to use Verko?
Creating an account, browsing listings and messaging a provider are free. What you pay for the work itself is the price the provider has set and that the two of you agree on — Verko does not add an agency margin on top of it.
Who decides the price?
The provider does. Every listing shows their own rate, which is why two cleaners in the same city can quote differently for what looks like the same job. Read what the price includes before you agree — travel, materials and the size of the property all change it.
Are providers checked in any way?
Accounts are verified by phone number, and every profile carries a legitimacy score alongside reviews left by customers after real jobs. That is a useful signal, not a background check — you should still read the reviews and ask questions before letting someone into your home.
Do I need to install an app?
No. Verko runs in the browser at app.verko.lt and can be added to your home screen if you want it to behave like an app. This website shows a sample of what is live; searching, messaging and booking happen in the app itself.
Which parts of Lithuania are covered?
Coverage follows the providers rather than a fixed map. Each listing states the area that person is willing to travel to, so availability is best in the larger cities and thinner in smaller towns. Searching for your area is the quickest way to see what is nearby.
How do I become a provider?
Create an account, then publish a listing describing what you do, the area you cover and what you charge. You choose your own hours and can change or pause a listing whenever you like. Reviews accumulate on your profile as you complete jobs.
What if something goes wrong with a job?
Raise it with the provider first — most problems are a misunderstanding about scope and get settled in the chat. If that does not resolve it, contact us at info@verko.lt with the listing and the conversation, and leave an honest review so other customers can see what happened.
Does this website use cookies and show ads?
Yes. verko.lt carries third-party advertising, and ad providers may set cookies in your browser to measure and select what is shown. The details, and how to opt out, are set out on our advertising and cookies page.
